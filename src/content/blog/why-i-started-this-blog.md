Every developer should have a blog. Not for clout or SEO, but because writing is thinking made visible.

## Writing clarifies thinking

When you sit down to explain a concept, you quickly discover what you don't fully understand. The gaps become obvious. The hand-wavy parts demand precision.

This is the primary reason I started this blog: to force myself to think more clearly about the things I work on.

## A home on the internet

In an age of social media platforms that come and go, a personal website is a piece of the web that you control. It's your space, with your rules, your design, and your voice.

No algorithm decides who sees your content. No platform can take it down. It's just you and the reader.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Web development** — what I'm learning and building
- **Design** — thoughts on interfaces, typography, and user experience
- **Tools and workflows** — things that make me more productive

I don't have a strict publishing schedule. I'll write when I have something worth saying. Quality over quantity.
